## 3. What is Included in the Website (Detailed Scope of Deliverables)

The sale of this website constitutes a complete, turn-key digital solution comprising the following modules, components, and assets:

### **3.1 Architecture & Core Technology Stack**
* **Framework:** React 19 & TypeScript for type-safe, resilient code.
* **Routing & SSR:** TanStack Start & TanStack Router providing server-side rendering and sub-second page transitions.
* **Styling & UI:** Tailwind CSS v4 with custom color palette (Espresso, Terracotta, Olive, Brass, Ivory, and Cream).
* **Component Library:** Radix UI primitives with Lucide Icons for clean, modern visual cues.
* **Performance:** Static asset bundling, lazy-loading images, zero layout shifts, optimized for Core Web Vitals.
* **Edge Cloud Ready:** Configured for Nitro / Cloudflare Workers deployment, offering world-class speed with near-zero hosting expenses.

### **3.2 Complete Set of Website Sections & Interactive Features**
1. **Header & Navigation Bar:**
   * Responsive sticky navigation bar with subtle backdrop blur and scroll detection.
   * Mobile-responsive slide-out navigation drawer with quick call and reservation triggers.
   * Brand wordmark and subtitle ("MARTABAAN - Restaurant & Bakery").

2. **Hero Presentation Section:**
   * Full-viewport hero banner with rich zoom effect and ambient gradient overlay.
   * Sector 1, Greater Noida location indicator and bilingual Hindi branding (`मार्तबान`).
   * High-converting headline (*"A Little Tradition. A Lot of Flavour."*).
   * Dual primary action triggers: *Explore the Menu* and *Reserve a Table*.

3. **Digital Interactive Menu System:**
   * **Full Menu Card Viewer:** Interactive cards representing original physical menu cards with zoom and modal pop-up.
   * **Real-Time Category Filtering:** 10 curated tabs:
     * *House Special Beverages* (14 items including Chaach, Lassi, Shikanji, Thandai, Shakes)
     * *Soups & Raita* (10 items including Shorba, Manchow, Sweet Corn, Raita)
     * *Tikka Shikka* (12 items including Paneer Tikka, Dahi Ke Sholey, Veg Seekh Kebab)
     * *Chaap Tandoor Se* (10 items including Malai Chaap, Bhut Jolokia Chaap)
     * *Paneer & Dal* (12 items including Dal Makhani, Dal Bukhara, Shahi Paneer)
     * *Vegetables & Kofta* (10 items including Malai Kofta, Gobhi Adraki, Jeera Aloo)
     * *Shakahari Gravy Chaap* (10 signature dishes including Rogan Josh, Chettinad, Changezi)
     * *Pilafs & Biryani* (10 items including Ghar Jaisa Pulav, Vegetable & Kathal Biryani)
     * *Oriental Delights* (11 items including Manchurian, Fried Rice, Hakka Noodles)
     * *Meethe Me / Desserts* (8 items including Signature White Chocolate Tortino, Fondant, Halwa, Gulab Jamun)
   * **90+ catalogued dishes** transcribed with accurate Indian Rupee pricing.

4. **Dedicated Ordering & Delivery Hub:**
   * Direct **Call-to-Order** card with formatted telephonic connection.
   * Direct **Swiggy** ordering integration linking to Martabaan's official online listing.
   * **Zomato** ordering module pre-configured for instant activation upon link confirmation.

5. **Artisan Bakery & Patisserie Feature:**
   * Dedicated bakery showcase for Cakes, Pastries, Fresh Breads, and Cookies.
   * Direct "Bakery enquiries" telephone CTA button.

6. **Brand Storytelling & Heritage Section ("Our Story"):**
   * Curated brand narrative highlighting the cultural significance of the *Martabaan* jar—preserving traditional family flavors and hospitality.
   * Authentic interior photography display.

7. **Interactive Ambience & Culinary Gallery:**
   * Masonry-style responsive photo gallery covering exterior views, interior dining room, signature platters, and bakery treats.
   * Tagged badges distinguishing genuine restaurant photography from illustrative dishes.
   * Fullscreen modal lightbox viewer with keyboard arrow navigation and backdrop dismiss.

8. **Reputation & Review Showcase:**
   * Prominent display of 4.4/5 star rating aggregated from 253+ verified diners.
   * Direct link to Swiggy Dineout public listing for verified customer reviews.

9. **Visit, Contact, Opening Hours & Google Maps Integration:**
   * Detailed address display (Shop 4 & 5, Shree Brahma Square, Behind ACE City, Sector 1, Greater Noida).
   * Direct telephone click-to-dial links (+91 81782 33039).
   * Business hours schedule display.
   * Integrated Google Maps live interactive embed with grayscale aesthetic.
   * One-click "Get Directions" navigation button using Google Maps API.
   * One-click Google Reserve table reservation integration.

10. **Site Footer:**
    * Clean footer with brand wordmark, bilingual logo, copyright notice, and legal credits.

### **3.3 Centralized Content Management System (Code-Level)**

2. **Third-Party Open Source Libraries:** The software utilizes industry-standard open-source libraries (e.g., React, TanStack, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons) governed by standard MIT licenses. The Buyer is granted permanent, irrevocable rights to use, modify, and build upon these packages without royalty obligations.
3. **Brand Assets & Trademarks:** All trademarks, brand names, food names, logos, and specific imagery relating to Martabaan Restaurant & Bakery remain the exclusive property of Martabaan.
4. **Portfolio Display:** The Seller reserves the non-commercial right to showcase the completed website in developer design portfolios, case studies, and resumes as an example of professional work.

### **4.2 Hosting, Domain & Recurring Operating Expenses**
1. **Domain Name:** Martabaan is responsible for purchasing, maintaining, and renewing its custom domain name (e.g., `martabaan.in` or `martabaan.com`) via a domain registrar (e.g., Cloudflare, GoDaddy, Hostinger).
2. **Hosting Infrastructure:** The website is configured to run on **Cloudflare Workers / Pages**. Cloudflare provides a generous free tier (handling up to 100,000 requests/day at zero cost under standard usage limits). Any potential future infrastructure scaling or third-party server fees remain the sole responsibility of the Buyer.
3. **No Hidden Monthly Agency Fees:** There are no mandatory monthly software subscription or licensing fees payable to the Seller. The purchase is a one-time capital acquisition.

### **4.3 Post-Handover Warranty & Support**
1. **Complimentary 14-Day Support:** The purchase includes **14 calendar days of hyper-responsive post-handover support** commencing on the date of final payment. Support covers:
   * Assistance with custom domain DNS connection.
   * Rectification of any software bugs, broken links, or display glitches.
   * Minor text and pricing adjustments in `src/data/site.ts`.
   * Guidance via WhatsApp, phone, or email.
2. **Exclusions from Complimentary Support:**
   * Major functional overhauls, database additions, or complete redesigns requested after handover sign-off.
   * Issues arising from unauthorized code tampering or deletion of core project files by third parties.
3. **Subsequent Maintenance:** After the 14-day warranty expires, any ongoing maintenance, major menu updates, or new feature additions can be engaged on an ad-hoc hourly rate (₹1,500/hr) or a monthly maintenance retainer agreement as mutually agreed.

### **4.4 Accuracy of Data & Compliance**
1. **Client Approval of Content:** All menu items, pricing, opening hours, and contact details have been structured to reflect the client's provided details. Martabaan assumes full responsibility for ensuring menu pricing accuracy and compliance with local regulatory requirements (FSSAI licensing, GST disclosures, etc.).
2. **Third-Party Platforms:** Delivery and reservation links (Swiggy, Zomato, Google Reserve) direct users to third-party platforms. The Seller is not responsible for platform commission structures, external downtime, or disputes arising on external partner platforms.

### **4.5 Limitation of Liability**
In no event shall the Seller be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages (including loss of restaurant revenue or data interruption) arising out of the operation or inability to operate the website once handed over to the Buyer. The Seller's total liability under any circumstance shall not exceed the total amount paid under this agreement (₹50,000).
